MEN OF PURPOSE: BIBLE STORIES AND PERSONAL REFLECTION

1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Open https://github.com/jovaughnhamilton-sys/Men-with-purpose
3. Choose Add file, then Upload files.
4. Upload index.html, mop-languages.js and sw.js into the repository root on main. Replace the existing files and commit.
5. Wait for GitHub Pages to finish publishing, then close and reopen the app and refresh.

Includes matching Bible stories and takeaways for the seven complete devotionals, plus personal reflection questions and a writing space on all 40 days. Saved reflections appear in My Prayer Journal and existing backups. Days 8 through 40 remain theme previews. Save a reflection before leaving its page.

Validated: all 40 day renders, seven stories, saving and updating reflections, reopening saved text, safe text rendering, journal labels, backup inclusion and deletion. Visual browser verification was not performed.
