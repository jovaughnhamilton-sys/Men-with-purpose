# January | Made New in Christ
Created Before You Achieved|Genesis 1|Before the bills and expectations, you are a person God made. God calls creation good before any human has earned a wage or a title. Your value begins with being His creation. Let that truth shape how you speak to yourself and how you treat the person who seems unable to offer you anything.|Treat someone with dignity without expecting a favor in return.
Work and Rest Belong Together|Genesis 2|You can care about your work and still need to stop. God gives the man meaningful work in a garden and establishes rest in the creation story. Responsibility and dependence belong together. You do not need to neglect your body or relationships to prove that you are useful.|Choose a stopping time for work and keep it today.
Come Out of Hiding|Genesis 3|Maybe you have been carrying an apology you keep postponing. After disobeying, the man and woman hide and blame. Shame still tempts us to conceal what needs attention. Your Brother Matters|Genesis 4|It can hurt to watch someone else receive what you wanted. Cain's anger becomes violence, and his question about his brother exposes a broken relationship. Walking Beyond a Legacy|Genesis 5|What will the people you love remember about your presence? A long list of generations reminds us that our lives are brief parts of a larger story. Faithfulness in a Corrupt World|Genesis 6|Doing the right thing can feel lonely when shortcuts are celebrated. Noah's obedience stands against the violence around him. Waiting Behind a Closed Door|Genesis 7|Waiting can make you wonder whether your effort matters. Noah enters the ark before the waters rise. Do Not Rush the Ground|Genesis 8|Relief can make you say yes before you are ready. Noah waits for signs that the earth is ready before leaving the ark. Relief can make us hurry into the next thing. Remembering Mercy|Genesis 9|A fresh start can still expose an old weakness. God's covenant with Noah follows judgment and a fresh beginning. One Family of Humanity|Genesis 10|It is easy to care most about people who resemble you. The nations spread across the earth through families and languages. A Name Worth Building|Genesis 11|You can reach a goal and still feel unseen. Babel's builders seek a name for themselves, but their project cannot secure their future. Leaving Familiar Ground|Genesis 12|Starting over can make even a capable man feel uncertain. Abram is called to leave what he knows, yet the same chapter records his fearful deception. Enough Room for Peace|Genesis 13|You do not have to win every decision in your home. Abram allows Lot to choose where to settle rather than letting a dispute between their herdsmen deepen. Honest Questions Before God|Genesis 15|You can love God and still have honest questions. Abram brings his unanswered concern about an heir directly to God. Faith is not the absence of questions. Beginning Again Tomorrow|2 Corinthians 5:14-21|You are allowed to begin again without denying what happened. New creation is connected to Christ's reconciling work and a changed way of seeing people.
